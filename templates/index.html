About Natal Astrology

Natal astrology is the system of astrology based on the concept that each person's personality or path in life can be determined by constructing a natal chart for the exact date, time, and place of a person's birth.

A natal chart, also called a birth chart, is a map of where all the planets were in their journey around the Sun at the exact moment you were born. This chart is calculated using the precise time, date, and place of your birth.

Features

  • Accurate planetary positions using Swiss Ephemeris data
  • Support for multiple house systems including Placidus, Koch, Whole Sign, and more
  • Detailed aspect calculations between planets
  • Automatic timezone detection from geographic coordinates
  • Interpretations of planets in signs, houses, and aspects
  • Comprehensive API for developers
