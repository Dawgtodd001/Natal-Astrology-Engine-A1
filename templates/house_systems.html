About House Systems

House systems are methods used in astrology to divide the celestial sphere into twelve sectors, called houses. Each house represents different areas of life and experience.

Primary House System Categories

Quadrant Systems

These divide the celestial sphere into four quadrants using the ascendant-descendant and midheaven-IC axes, and then divide each quadrant into three houses. Examples: Placidus, Koch, Campanus, Regiomontanus

Equal House Systems

These create twelve equal divisions of the ecliptic, usually starting from the ascendant. Examples: Equal House, Whole Sign

Other Systems

Some systems use other astronomical points or mathematical calculations. Examples: Porphyry, Morinus

Choosing a House System

The choice of house system depends on:

  • The astrological tradition you follow
  • Personal preference and experience
  • The specific type of astrological work you're doing
  • Geographical considerations (some systems work poorly at extreme latitudes)

Many modern astrologers use Placidus (the default in our application), while traditional astrologers often prefer Whole Sign or Regiomontanus. Equal house is popular for its simplicity.
